# Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 13, 2026

Power Trip Electrical ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal data when you use our website or contact us.

## Information we collect

When you contact us, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, postcode/area, and details about your enquiry. We may also collect basic website usage information such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited.

## How we use your information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, provide quotes, schedule work, carry out electrical services, and improve our website and service delivery.

## Sharing information

We do not sell personal information. We only share information with trusted service providers where required to run our business (for example, hosting or communication tools), or where disclosure is required by law.

## Data retention

We keep personal data only as long as reasonably necessary for enquiry handling, service delivery, record-keeping, and legal obligations.

## Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

## Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data where applicable. To make a request, contact [hello@powertripelectrical.co.uk](mailto:hello@powertripelectrical.co.uk).

## Contact
