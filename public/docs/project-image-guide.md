## Suggested prompts for generated photos

1. Hero: A professional electrician in a clean modern domestic setting, standing near a consumer unit and modern lighting installation, realistic photography style, natural lighting, trustworthy local trade website aesthetic.
2. Hero support: Close-up of a modern consumer unit with testing tools in use, realistic trade photography, tidy and professional environment.
3. Project 01: Full rewire in a 1930s family home, electrician working carefully, neat cable routes, realistic residential interior.
4. Project 02: Consumer unit and kitchen lighting upgrade, finished kitchen with warm downlights and practical task lighting.
5. Project 03: Neatly installed EV charger on the exterior wall of a detached UK home with visible driveway.
6. Project 04: Outdoor security and pathway lighting at dusk, clean exterior and believable residential installation.
7. Project 05: Small office electrical fit-out with modern LED lighting and tidy power/data points.
8. Project 06: Landlord remedial electrical works in rental flat, electrician testing sockets and final checks before handover.
